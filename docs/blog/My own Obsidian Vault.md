It's been roughly half a year since I started using **[Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/)**, it's a great tool, but I just felt like I wasn't utilizing it 100%. That's why a week ago, i decided to finally work on my own system, it was the right decision looking back.

What inspired me was a video by **Karlos** about [Obsidian's CEO, Kepano's vault design](https://youtu.be/Dq3R3uS0sQ4?list=TLPQMjkwNzIwMjZ46vUcuqSvbw), his vault was truly mind bending and chaotic, the idea of putting *most notes at root* was weird at that time, but that idea struck me. It wasn't all that weird is it, most times things just don't belong *solely* to **one single folder**, instead of debating which folder should the note be, Kepano's philosophy being **fast and lazy** was really refreshing.

I took it as an inspiration, a base point to design my own system. I like the idea of putting notes at root, and use **categories and bases** instead of tags to link stuff, but I still need some **structure**, my vault isn't only for journal, it also handles my uni notes, my project documentation that I use scripts to output *(I just put it here for convenience sake)*, and etc. So I still use some folders for notes, most of them are still in roots. I also adopt linking stuff, having notes about people I know, notes about places around me or games I play, and much much more. In terms of plugins, Kepano's entire vault only use templater as of community plugins, I *can* do that, however certain stuff just feels nice to have, like a dedicated **task panel** instead of putting a file as panel, or **day planner** for scheduling, and also **calendar** and **periodic notes**. In the end, I have a few extra plugins, but I don't abuse them, definitely not bloating my vault with 100 not used plugins. At first, I also included **Dataview** due to 1 specific table I needed, the rest I use **Obsidian bases**, which are faster but less powerful. Luckily I was able to remove Dataview with some workaround, though not perfect, it's usable.

That's the brief story of me starting my own system, it's simple but works.

*p.s. really don't understand why some spend tens of thousands of hours designing their system when they could spend all that time actually using Obsidian*
