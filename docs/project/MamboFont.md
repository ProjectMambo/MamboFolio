## In The Beginning 

### What Was Missing?
It all started during China's Song Dynasty, around mid 11th century, **Bi Sheng** invented moving type printing, making a huge leap in printing technology. Four centuries later, a German guy called Johannes Gutenberg invented movable-type printing press, another milestone in typography. Then there was Nicolas Jenson, the inventor of Roman type, a better alternative to Gutenberg's Blackletter, as well as the ancestors of a great portion of modern fonts, most notably Centaur.

"If I have seen further, it is by standing on the shoulders of giants.", originally from Bernard of Chartres, and made famous by Sir Isaac Newton *(though it was kind of an insult to Robert Hooke)*, really speaks itself. Most aspects of human civilisation are built on our ancestors' founding, that includes typography, the art of making written characters readable.
